# The solid box fault stays

The three-byte entity box of `notes/2026-09-29/solid_encode.md` is retained as the engine and Raven have it. Ruled 2026-10-29.

The port matches the oracle. The pack of the box into `entityState_t.solid` is engine work at `sv_world.cpp:194-231`, the client rebuild is cgame work at `cg_predict.c:218-260`, and base MP produces no box the three bytes cannot carry. A link-time snap in the module would be a change with no Raven counterpart and no base trigger, which rule 19 does not cover. The open rulings of `notes/2026-09-29/solid_encode-resume.md` close with this one: the fix, if any, belongs to a mod that scales breakables, not to this port.
