## Findings with no ruling

- A slot write does not fill with zeros as `Q_strncpyz` does (`Sources/JKAShared/Views/CStrings.swift:14-20`, Raven `q_shared.c:838`). After the fix of `SV_Netchan_Decode`, an empty command over a longer old command can give a wrong decode key. No trap.
- `Info_SetValueForKey` traps for a text of exactly 1024 bytes, where Raven's `strcat` writes one byte past the buffer (`q_shared.c.swift:1286-1292`, Raven `q_shared.c:1312-1318`). A crafted `getstatus` or `connect` packet can reach it.
- The Swift `ref_dump` prints its hex in a form that `referee.py` cannot parse (`sv_referee.swift:685-709`).
- The referee checksums depend on how the frames are grouped into `ref_step` calls, on both hosts.
- The start of the server spends its time in `MSG_initHuffman` (28.8 million `Huff_addRef` calls, as in Raven and OpenJK) and in the load of the string packages. The lag on a swoop in the live test is not measured.
- The game logic has no review for an index that Raven leaves at -1. The host side of the seam has one, and it found no other site.
