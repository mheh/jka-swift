Second, the gap lines now follow three conventions: a `comments` row of S3 binds a gap to the next statement, a nested comment row of `lines` binds to the innermost enclosing statement, which finding 31 of the step-4 review carries to steps 8 and 9, and a nested blank row binds to the body. One rule for every gap line is what the align and compare passes need, and that rule is finding 31's to settle. Third, S22's compare prints the C line of `statements.start_line` for an edited or hand line, and a blank under seq 0 cites the function's opening brace, the least useful line, where its enclosing statement's line sits beside it. S18's align matches stripped lines with difflib and reads no `statement_seq`, so neither form harms it.

The fix. The blank tests of the walk read a bare line, at `draft_statements.py:42-45`, `144-147`, `846`, `862`, `872`, `1019`, and `1038-1047`, so a marked blank would fail them today, which is the reason the builder gave.
