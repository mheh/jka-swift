# Stage two of the table cache, the proposed defaults

The rulings C1 to C9 of `notes/2026-10-05/table-cache.md` stand, and this docket proposes the defaults under them. One row is one ruling. The columns are the row, the question, the proposed default, and the reason.

What I measured. A cold `bodies.py --stem bg_pmove` takes 74.6 seconds and a warm one 26.7. A warm `--only PM_AddTouchEnt` takes 21.4, so the fixed cost of a run is about 21 seconds, the parse, the skeleton, and the registry, and the draft of the 8,968 lines of bg_pmove takes about 5. The last gate left 85 drafts under `.build-draft/drafts/` with 137,062 lines, 1,468 Port-notes, and 1,038 `// Raven wrote:` lines. 91 of the 92 stubs under `Sources/JKAMP_BG` and `Sources/JKAMP_GameLogic` are adopted, so the align pass covers 91 hand files. The fast tool tests run in 1.6 seconds, 23 tests, and `test_lookup` fails on a fresh store until `manifest.py` runs once, which is the first stage-one carry-over.

What I read. The drafter is `bodies.py` and the five `draft_*.py` modules, 4,230 lines, with 85 `raise Hole` sites, 58 sites that join Raven's trailing comment onto the statement's own line, 20 reads of the registry, 43 reads of `spelling.py`, and 23 reads of the views. The drafter already keys every line to its statement through `STATEMENT_MARK` and `statement_key`, the tuple of the function name, the cursor kind, and the byte extent, and `draftcheck.py` reads that key to turn a rejected statement into a compile hole on the next pass. c2rust builds a control-flow graph for every body, so it has no CFG-free statement path, and its line map is one `c2rust::src_loc` attribute per item, not per statement. Its `WithStmts` is the expression-level lifting that `pending` does here.

Two facts decide the shape. First, the drafter spells every expression in Swift while it walks the cursor, through `spelling.py`, the views, and the registry, so the lowered rows carry destination text per statement and not a language-free tree. A tree is a rewrite of the 976 lines of `draft_expressions.py` and the 531 of `draft_calls.py` that no byte-for-byte gate can hold midway. Second, the registry reads every Swift file under `Sources/`, so the lowered rows need a third key generation, because the adoption key covers the first line of each stub alone.

| S7 | The line rows, the printed side of the line map. | One table `lines`, in the lowered generation. Columns: `language`, `file`, `function`, `seq`, `statement_seq`, `role`, `depth`, `text`. `seq` numbers the lines of one function from its first header line to its closing brace. `statement_seq` is null for a header line and for the function's own braces. The lowering writes every line the printer prints, the three hole lines included, with no indentation in `text`. | A line map that misses the hole lines cannot align a hand file, because the hand file keeps them. | S8 | The role set. | `header` for the comment block, the Source line, and the signature. `own` for a line the statement's own text gives. `lifted` for a line that runs before its statement, a scratch local, a lifted store, or a note from inside an expression. `trailing` for Raven's comment at the end of a statement's line. `comment` and `blank` for a gap line. `frame` for a brace, a `do {`, a `while true {`, a closure head, or a `break` the form adds. `copy` for a parameter copy and `hoist` for a shared read at the block head. `text` for Raven's text kept as a comment, the `commented` arm. `note`, `raven`, `marker` for the three lines of a hole or the two of a note. | Each role names one emitter in the drafter today, so the builder adds a role at each emitter and nothing else changes. | S9 | How a hole is a row. | One `lowered` row with form `hole`, its `reason`, and `message` for a compile hole, plus three `lines` rows with roles `note`, `raven`, `marker` under the same `statement_seq`. A declaration hole inside a `DECL_STMT` is one hole row for the statement with the variable name in the reason. | The three-line form is the transcription contract, and the lines rows keep it byte for byte. | S10 | How a Port-note binds to a C line. | Through `statement_seq`. A note's `lines` row carries the `statement_seq` of the statement it stands above, and `statements.start_line` is the C line. A note from inside an expression binds to the enclosing statement, as `pending` does today. A note on the function itself has null `statement_seq` and binds to the function's first line through `header`. | The drafter has no expression key, and C9 keeps the statement as the unit. | S11 | The trailing comment. | The lowering writes the statement's own line and the trailing comment as two `lines` rows, roles `own` and `trailing`, and the printer joins a `trailing` row onto the line before it with one space. | 58 sites append the comment to the text today, and the align pass needs the comment as its own fact to match a hand line that moved it. | S12 | The boundary between the lowering pass and the printer. | The lowering pass is the five `draft_*.py` modules under `bodies.py`, renamed to `lower_*.py` in the last step, and it writes `lowered`, `lines`, and `macros` rows. ## Open findings

The lookup test fails on a fresh store. `bodies.py` created `table.sqlite` in my run, and the store holds no signature rows until `manifest.py` runs, so `test_lookup` reports `no function named Pmove`. HANDOFF names the fix as the first carry-over, and S25 takes it.

c2rust has no CFG-free statement translation. `cfg::Cfg::from_stmts` builds a graph for every body and the relooper rebuilds the structure, and `convert_stmt_help` opens a work-in-progress block per statement. The part that is free of the graph is the expression lifting of `WithStmts`, which collects side-effect statements beside a value, the same shape as `pending` here. Its line map is one `c2rust::src_loc = "line:col"` attribute per item through `add_src_loc_attr`, and no statement carries one. Its comments live in a `CommentStore` keyed by the position of the node that follows the comment, with trailing comments on statements alone, which is the `above`, `gap`, and `trailing` split of S3. Its function-like macro path, `convert_fn_macro_invocation`, re-emits the macro name and the argument text as a Rust macro call, with no table of where the macro expanded.

The Port-note count of the drafts, 1,468, is 19 above the gate's 1,449 holes. The difference is the plain notes the drafter writes without a `portMissing` marker, such as the repeated-test note and the first-field note of a format argument. S8 gives those lines the role `lifted`, and S6 gives them no `lowered` row, so the counts stay apart.

The fixed cost of a drafter run is 21 seconds warm. The parse is under 2, so the skeleton's own walk and the registry's regex pass over `Sources/` carry the rest. S15 removes the whole cost for a printer-only read, and a signature table for the registry, the stage-three hook, would remove most of it for the lowering too.

The `statements` text column repeats Raven's source. At one row per statement over both units the table is about the size of the game folder's C text, under 10 megabytes, and the store grows from 1 megabyte to about 15 with the `lines` rows of 85 stems.

The `test_project.py` test reads the module files at commit `95fb8c0` through `git show`. It stays valid through stage two, and it is the one test that needs the git history.
